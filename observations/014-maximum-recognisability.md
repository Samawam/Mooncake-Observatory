# Maximum Recognisability

Researchers conducting editorial review identified a recurring preference.

Observations were not consistently judged according to seriousness.

Nor were they judged according to complexity.

Instead, a different question repeatedly emerged.

"Does this still sound like the Observatory?"

Further investigation suggests that recognisability may be more valuable than perfection.

An imperfect observation that remains recognisably itself is preferable to a flawless observation that could have originated anywhere.

## Classification

Editorial Principle

## Additional Observation

The purpose of refinement is not to remove identity.

The purpose of refinement is to make identity easier to recognise.
