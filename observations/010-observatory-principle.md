# The Observatory Principle

Early discussions about the purpose of cairns produced several findings.

A good idea may be worth saving.

A finished observation may be worth finding.

These statements appear related, but they are not identical.

Researchers note that a cairn is not intended to represent perfection.

Its purpose is not to provide a final answer.

Its purpose is to indicate that something was found here.

Further travellers may continue the journey, revisit the location, or choose a different route entirely.

## Classification

Observatory Principle

## Additional Observation

A cairn does not mark the end of a path.

It merely confirms that a path existed.
