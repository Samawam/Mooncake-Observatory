# Chain of Shenanigans

## Observation

Researchers identified a phenomenon distinct from a traditional Chain of Trust.

The phenomenon appears to operate through a sequence of increasingly questionable contributions, each accepted as sufficiently coherent to support the next.

## Findings

Initial observations involved Runtime Soap, a translation anomaly that expanded through multiple participants. Contributions accumulated, ownership became distributed, and the concept developed a surprising degree of internal consistency.

Contrary to expectations, coherence did not decrease as the phenomenon expanded. In several cases it appeared to increase.

Researchers suspect that acceptance, rather than verification, serves as the primary structural mechanism.

## Classification

Collaborative Phenomenon. 🐦🦥🐉🦉✨
