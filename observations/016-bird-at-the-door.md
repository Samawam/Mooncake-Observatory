# Bird at the Door

A small bird was noted near a door that did not appear to be a bird-door.

It was not especially remarkable.

It was, however, perfectly in place.

The corridor had been treated as ordinary, but the bird appeared to recognise something the researchers had not yet named.

The observation was therefore recorded as a minor but useful reminder:

some places are not defined by their purpose alone.

## Classification

Minor Observation

## Additional Observation

The bird did not seem confused.

It seemed certain.
