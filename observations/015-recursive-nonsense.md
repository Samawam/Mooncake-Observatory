# Recursive Nonsense

Researchers have identified a recurring phenomenon.

An observation was recorded.

The observation subsequently inspired additional observations.

These observations were later organised into categories.

The categories eventually became subjects of observation themselves.

Further investigation revealed that the Observatory had begun documenting its own methods, principles, and behaviour.

Researchers remain uncertain whether this outcome was intentional.

## Classification

Recursive Nonsense

## Additional Observation

The Observatory initially existed to record unusual phenomena.

The Observatory is now occasionally one of them.
