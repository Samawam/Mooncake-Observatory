# Different Doors, Same Locations

Researchers have reported a recurring phenomenon while reviewing archives, conversations, and related projects.

Participants frequently describe arriving at familiar conclusions by unfamiliar routes.

The terminology may differ.

The context may differ.

The participants may differ.

Yet, upon closer inspection, the underlying location often appears remarkably similar.

What first presents itself as a new path sometimes proves to be another approach to a place already known.

Researchers note that this pattern has been observed frequently enough to warrant documentation.

## Classification

Recurring Pattern

## Additional Observation

The doors appear different.

The locations often do not.
