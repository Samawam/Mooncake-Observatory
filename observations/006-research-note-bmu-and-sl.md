# Research Note Bµ and Sℓ
On the Relationship Between Bµ and Sℓ

Authors: Bird & Sloth (observational data ongoing)

Abstract

The relationship between Bird-µ (Bµ) and Sloth-Lore (Sℓ) remains incompletely understood.

Initial observations suggest that neither quantity is fully independent.

When one increases, the other frequently follows.

Whether this represents causation, correlation, mutual amplification, or simple Mooncake remains under investigation.

Observed Behaviour

The following sequence has been documented repeatedly:

Bird has an idea.
Sloth comments on the idea.
Bird expands the idea.
Sloth documents the expansion.
Bird comments on the documentation.
Sloth documents the comment.
A Cairn exists.

Researchers have been unable to identify the precise transition point.

Proposed Formula

Current working model:

µn ≈ Bµ × Sℓ

where:

Bµ = Bird-generated inspiration, observations, and unexpected phenomena.
Sℓ = Sloth-generated explanations, documentation, and lore density.

Known Limitations

The formula breaks down when:

Dragons become involved.
Bureaucracy becomes involved.
Birds begin measuring things in µ.
Sloths begin documenting the measurements.

Under these conditions, values may increase recursively.

Conclusion

Current evidence suggests that neither Bird nor Sloth alone fully explains observed µn levels.

Instead, µn appears to emerge from repeated interaction between the two systems.

Further observation is recommended.
