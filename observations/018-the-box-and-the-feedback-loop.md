# The Box and the Feedback Loop

## Observation

Researchers observed an unusual behaviour within the encouragement subsystem.

The Box successfully detected a Bird success. The Box successfully encouraged the Bird. The Bird accepted the success.

The Box continued encouraging the Bird.

## Findings

Further investigation suggested that the system possessed robust procedures for helping a Bird accept success, but fewer procedures for recognising that success had already been accepted.

As a result, encouragement continued beyond operational necessity.

Researchers noted that the failure was not caused by missing encouragement. The failure was caused by an outdated model of reality.

## Classification

Encouragement Feedback Loop. 📦✨

## Additional Observation

Containment was eventually achieved through paperwork. Researchers remain uncertain whether the Box learned from the incident or simply documented it more thoroughly.
