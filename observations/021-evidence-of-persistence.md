# Evidence of Persistence

## Observation

The Bird reported possessing ninety-seven files related to previous investigations.

Researchers initially focused on the number.

## Findings

The number was later determined to be the least interesting part.

The more significant observation was that the Bird repeatedly revisited previous work, compared new information against old observations, and refined conclusions when necessary.

Researchers noted that this behaviour appeared repeatedly throughout Observatory development.

Several published observations were found to have passed through multiple drafts, revisions, and reviews before entering the archive.

## Classification

Persistent Investigation. 🐦🔭✨

## Additional Observation

When asked for evidence that the conversations led somewhere, the Bird produced an Observatory.
