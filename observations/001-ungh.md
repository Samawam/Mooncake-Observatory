# Ungh (µ)

Symbol: µ

Pronunciation: "ungh"

A unit used to estimate effort, resistance,
inconvenience, mental friction, emotional weight,
or general ungh.

Unlike many conventional units, µ is not intended
to be precise.

It is intended to be understood.

## Examples

Learning a new tool: ~6µ

Fixing a bug in five minutes: ~1µ

Fixing a bug after searching for three hours: ~11µ

Accidentally creating an entire universe of citizens,
observatories, councils, traditions, and measurements:
∞µ (disputed)

## Observation

A curious phenomenon occurs when µ is applied to meaningful things.

A project may begin at 12µ.

Then become 7µ.

Then 4µ.

Then somehow remain a great deal of work while simultaneously
becoming something one looks forward to.

Current theories suggest that purpose, curiosity,
friendship, birds, and excessive amounts of mooncake
may alter observed µ values.

Further observation required.
