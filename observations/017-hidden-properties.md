# Hidden Properties

Researchers have noted that some characteristics remain difficult to detect during ordinary inspection.

They are not absent.

They are simply not yet legible from the angle at hand.

Casual observers may therefore conclude that nothing unusual is present, and in many cases that conclusion is understandable.

The property did not announce itself.

Further consideration suggests that certain features are not hidden in the sense of being missing, but hidden in the sense of waiting for the right context.

The object remains unchanged.

Only the frame of understanding shifts.

## Classification

Unrevealed Characteristic

## Additional Observation

The absence of visible evidence is not necessarily evidence of absence.

Some properties simply wait for the right angle of attention. 🐦🔭✨
